Hermes planner

Hermes planner is a private, single-user tool. It lets the owner's personal assistant (running on the owner's own server) read the owner's Google Calendar, add events to its own "Hermes" calendar, add and complete items in the owner's Google Tasks, and read spreadsheets the owner points it to in Google Sheets.

It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it.
